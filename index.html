Read the welcome

Welcome to Leadership Redefined. At the Symposium you met your pod and heard from Chancellor Diermeier and Vanderbilt’s leaders. Over the next three weeks, you put those ideas to work. Here is what to do. In week two, starting November second, take Course One. It is self-paced and online, and covers the Chancellor’s three modules: vision and strategy, mission and margin, and reputational stewardship. To begin, select Start Course One on this page. The course saves your place in this browser, so you can stop and come back. Inside, select Listen to hear each page, or Auto to have every page read to you. By Friday, November sixth, post your response to discussion prompt one in your cohort’s Teams channel, and reply to at least one colleague. In week three, starting November ninth, take Course Two: communication and brand, the entrepreneurial mindset, and high-performing teams. Discussion prompt two is due Friday, November thirteenth. Through all of it, your pod writes one capstone brief: seven sections, about three pages. Course One helps you draft sections one to three, and Course Two, sections four to seven. Everything you write collects in Your capstone brief, where you can edit it, print it, or download it for Word. In week four, your pod finishes the brief together and turns it in by Friday, November twentieth. Your cohort’s Teams channel is your home base: that is where you post the discussion prompts, and where to ask any questions along the way. Each course links to it. Now select Start Course One to begin. We are excited to have you in the program.
