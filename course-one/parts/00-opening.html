The belief

A vision only matters when people can act on it. Leadership Redefined exists so that the leaders who run Vanderbilt every day can see the strategy, tell its story, and fund it on purpose.

The institutional why · Crescere aude

Vanderbilt will define the great university of the 21st Century and be it.

That is Chancellor Diermeier’s institutional vision, one sentence with no hedge in it. The how is equally blunt: operate at uncommon speed, agility, and scale to address society’s challenges through education and research, and help reclaim the essential role of leading research universities in driving American prosperity. Our motto has always been the dare, Crescere aude, dare to grow.

Areas of Focus

Three ways Vanderbilt delivers on the vision

1

Exceptional Core Operations

With speed, agility and scale, Vanderbilt will distinguish itself with innovative and impactful capabilities to ensure that we have the talent, resources and reputation necessary to deliver on our vision.

2

Bold Strategic Initiatives

With “exceptional core operations” as our fuel, Vanderbilt will pursue increasingly bold initiatives that enable and enhance the reach and impact of our education and research mission.

3

Values Leadership

Through strategic advocacy efforts and modeling the role of an essential research university, Vanderbilt will stimulate industry change and combat existential threats to our education and research mission.

How this course delivers

These three areas are also your capstone’s three focus areas. This week you learn to read the strategy closely enough to pick one on purpose, to tell the story of your challenge so others want to act, and to make the case in both mission and money. That is how a pod’s brief becomes something the university can actually fund.

The reputation flywheel

Reputation and resources feed each other in a loop, reputation attracts talent, funding, partnerships, and access, which build the reputation. The university spins that flywheel with execution with ambition, values-driven leadership, and athletics momentum. Here’s your place in it: a leader who can say in one sentence how their team’s work serves the vision, and back it with a clear story and an honest budget, is execution with ambition in person. Every brief that ties a real problem to the strategy feeds the wheel from your desk.
