Choose your focus area

Your brief is judged only against the other briefs in its focus area, so the choice matters. Read each scenario and decide where it belongs. The point is to practice the call your pod will make.

You’ll doSort five challenges into the three focus areas Time4 min
The idea

Exceptional Core Operations is about how well Vanderbilt runs: speed, agility, and scale in the work every day. Bold Strategic Initiatives are the new bets that extend the reach of education and research. Values Leadership is how Vanderbilt models the role of an essential research university and speaks up for it. Plenty of good ideas touch all three; pick the one where your solution does the most work.

1/5New staff wait three to five weeks for system access, so their first month is mostly waiting.

2/5A cross-school program that pairs Nashville employers with student researchers on regional health problems.

3/5A shared practice for hosting events where people who disagree sharply can still hear each other out.

4/5Three offices each collect the same vendor data in three different forms.

5/5Turning a successful pilot in one school into a university-wide offering for working adults.
