A strong example.

An invented example, for practice: using gigs to cut what we spend on outside contractors.

The case · Sections 1 to 3

1 Focus area
Exceptional Core Operations.
2 The challenge
Departments pay contractors and consultants for short projects, like data analysis and web updates, that our own staff could do. The money leaves, and staff who want to grow go elsewhere.
3 Connection
The vision asks us to operate with speed, agility and scale. Using skills we already have keeps fees inside Vanderbilt.

The plan · Sections 4 to 7

4 Solution
People: managers share up to 10% of a staff member’s time for a gig. Process: before hiring outside help for a short project, post it as a gig for two weeks. Technology: gigs are posted and matched in the Talent Marketplace.
5 Steps
Weeks 1 to 4, set the gig rules with Procurement (HR lead). Weeks 5 to 16, pilot in two divisions. Week 17, review results with sponsors.
6 Metrics
Contractor and consulting spend in the pilot divisions drops 15% from last year by June. Staff fill 25 gigs.
7 Sustainability
HR’s Talent Marketplace team owns it after the pilot. The gig check becomes a standard step before hiring a contractor.
