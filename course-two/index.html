    Lesson 1 of 4 · Know your problem

    Analyze it, or test it?

    The right move depends on the kind of problem. Most capstones are complex: run a pilot.

    The four kinds
    • Simple: a known best practice. Sense, categorize, respond.
    • Complicated: a right answer experts can find. Sense, analyze, respond.
    • Complex: no answer up front. Probe, sense, respond.
    • Chaotic: act first to restore order. Act, sense, respond.

    Snowden and Boone, A Leader’s Framework for Decision Making, HBR, 2007. The program’s pre-work.
