Studio B

Strong metric or weak metric?

“Success metrics” is where many briefs go soft. A strong metric names what changes, by how much, for whom, and by when. Sort six.

You’ll doSort six metrics Time4 min

1/6“Increase awareness of the new process.”

2/6“Median days from hire to full system access drops from 21 to 5 by the end of spring semester.”

3/6“Launch the new website.”

4/6“At least 70% of staff use the tool in year one.”

5/6“Number of emails sent promoting the program.”

6/6“Pilot event attendance rises 25% over last spring, and 60% of attendees return for a second event.”
