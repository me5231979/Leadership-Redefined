Capstone Innovation Project

Name a challenge. Solve it with all six.

Your pod submits a concise brief that names a critical challenge or opportunity for Vanderbilt and solves it using all six program topics. Pick one focus area: Exceptional Core Operations, Bold Strategic Initiatives, or Values Leadership. Three winners will be named, one in each focus area, and your brief is judged within the area you choose, so state it clearly at the top.

Your brief must cover
  1. Your focus area
  2. The challenge and why it matters strategically
  3. Connection to Vanderbilt’s priorities
  4. An integrated solution across people, process, and technology
  5. Implementation steps, owners, and timeline
  6. Success metrics
  7. A sustainability plan

No longer than three pages. A panel of judges reviews every brief and awards winners using a rubric tied to these requirements. Due Friday, November 20.
