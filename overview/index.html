02 What

What the program is.

Two online courses

Each takes about 45 minutes, at your own pace. They cover the six Symposium topics and show you how to use them with your team.

Course One: vision and strategy, mission and margin, and reputation. Course Two: communication and brand, the entrepreneurial mindset, and high-performing teams.

The capstone

Your pod picks a real problem at Vanderbilt and comes up with a way to fix it, using what you learn.

Pick one focus area: Exceptional Core Operations, Bold Strategic Initiatives, or Industry Leadership. There is one winner in each area.

The brief

A short paper, three pages at most, where your pod writes up its idea to share it.

The courses help you write it as you go. It is due Friday, November 20.

Coaching

You take an assessment, then book time with your coach whenever it suits you during the program.

You talk about what the program means for your job and where you want to grow.

03 How · Inside a course

Find your way around.

  1. Listen and Auto. Listen reads the page out loud. Auto reads each page for you, one after another.
  2. Mark done. Checks off the activity on that page.
  3. Dots and arrows. Go to the next page, or tap a dot to jump ahead.
  4. Progress. Shows what you have done and what is left. The course remembers where you stopped.
  5. Your brief and Print takeaway. Go to your brief, or print a one-page summary of the week.
  6. Read along. Shows the narration as text.

03 How · Your capstone

Your capstone brief.

Your pod picks a real problem at Vanderbilt and writes a short brief, three pages at most, about the problem and how to fix it. Here is what to do each week.

  1. Week of Nov 2. In Course One, write sections 1 to 3: your focus area, the problem, and why it matters to Vanderbilt.
  2. Week of Nov 9. In Course Two, write sections 4 to 7: your solution, the steps, how you will measure it, and how it will last.
  3. Week of Nov 16. Meet with your pod, put your drafts together, and turn in one brief by Friday, Nov 20.

What you type in the courses goes into your brief. From there you can print it or download it for Word. Use the same computer and browser each time, because your work is saved there.

03 How · Your assessment and coach

Work with your coach.

Your coaching time is a chance to talk through what the program means for you, the way you would with an executive coach.

  1. Take the assessment

    Your results give you and your coach a place to start.

  2. Schedule time with your coach

    You book your session, any time during the program. It is a one-on-one conversation about you and your work.

What you talk about
  • Your assessment results
  • How the program applies to your job
  • Where you want to grow as a leader
  • The challenges you are facing right now

04 Also for managers

Other programs to know.

HCM Academy

What it is: training and a community for HCMs. HCMs handle the people work in your area, like hiring, pay and timecards, transfers, leave, and records.

Why managers need it: your HCM helps with every hire, paycheck, and job change on your team. Know what they handle so the work goes to the right person.

Manager Voyage

What it is: one standard for every Vanderbilt manager, with a set path for your first 60 days.

Why managers need it: if you manage people, Oracle Learning assigns it to you. It includes required courses, a 35-minute course on the basics, and the Manager Portal.
